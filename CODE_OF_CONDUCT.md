# Community standards

ContriBolt welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Keep discussion useful, patient, and focused on the work.

- Give concrete feedback on code and behavior, not personal attributes.
- Respect maintainers' contribution rules and decisions, including policies about AI assistance.
- Protect personal information, credentials and private project data.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, impersonation, spam and unsolicited contribution campaigns.
- Do not represent fixture results, guesses or tool output as another person's endorsement.

Maintainers may edit/remove disruptive material or restrict participation to protect constructive collaboration. They are also expected to follow these standards.

For a conduct concern, use a private contact method explicitly listed on the [maintainer's profile](https://github.com/ayush-singh-0601). If none is listed, request a private channel without posting names or incident details. [Security reports](SECURITY.md) follow the separate security policy.
